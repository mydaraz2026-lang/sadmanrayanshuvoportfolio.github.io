Signature Project

Kansai Nerolac Bangladesh, 2019–2025: a corporate sales function built from zero brand recognition

Kansai Nerolac Paints Bangladesh · Deputy Manager, Corporate Sales

Building Market Trust From Zero: Winning Bangladesh's National Power Infrastructure Pipeline

When Kansai Nerolac Paints entered Bangladesh in 2019, it had no local track record and no brand recognition among the international EPC contractors driving the country's power infrastructure build-out. I built the corporate sales function from the ground up — no inherited accounts, no first-mover advantage, no reputation to lean on.

Foreign EPC contractors typically arrive with procurement shortlists already fixed, defaulting to brands they've worked with elsewhere. Winning meant becoming the person clients never had to chase — always reachable, always following through, always ahead of the problem rather than reacting to it. That consistency is what let procurement teams commit large-value orders to a brand with zero prior track record in the country.

Results

  • 100% of paint requirements won on 2 national landmark power projects — Barisal Coal-Fired Power Plant and Bangladesh Summit Meghnaghat Phase II (600MW CCGT) — the first time Kansai Nerolac Bangladesh achieved full-scope share on a landmark project
  • Scaled the same trust-first approach across 6 national landmark infrastructure projects, including Banshkhali 1,320MW, Patuakhali 1,320MW, and Matarbari 2×600MW Ultra Super Critical coal-fired plants, and the Cox's Bazar 60MW Wind Power Plant (US-DK Green Energy)
  • Single purchase order of BDT 50M (~USD $407K) for fireproof coatings — the highest-value single order in company history
  • Grew the corporate account base from 0 to 60+ medium and large corporate customers (25+ tier-1), and delivered 30% revenue growth in Year 1
